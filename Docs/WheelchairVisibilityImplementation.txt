Wheelchair visibility and refuge floor checkpoint
Date: 2026-11-22

Changes
- Escort defaults to 1.4m forward and 0.25m left. These are serialized tuning values on CampusWheelchairRescue.
- Candidate poses first reduce lateral offset from full to half to centered, then try 1.3m spacing. Unsafe paired player/chair poses are rejected.
- Separate planned heading from the offset position so the NPC does not turn diagonally merely because it is offset left. Head gaze does not move the chair.
- Active wheelchair/NPC colliders remain disabled and restored on pause/completion. Right trigger still teleports; A still starts/pauses escort, operates doors and confirms parking.
- Refuge parking moves to (4.85,3.65,-5.3), the far-left corner when looking into the room from its doorway. The parked person faces the doorway (+Z).
- Marker is a thin floor target over the parking pose; a downward arrow floats above it. Both floor and arrow accept the same right-ray A interaction.
- Waiting / ready / hovered / completed states remain gray / green / gold / cyan. Confirmation hides the arrow and disables both interaction targets.
- Label retains English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese text with highlighted A key.
- Proximity fading is deferred pending headset feedback, as proposed. Model proportions remain unchanged.
