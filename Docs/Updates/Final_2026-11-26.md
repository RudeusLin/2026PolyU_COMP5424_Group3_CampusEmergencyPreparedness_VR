# Final source version — 26 November 2026

Final approved version tag: `FINAL-20261126-R2`. The project owner confirmed that final testing met expectations. This version supersedes `FINAL-20261126`; the earlier tag is retained as a historical snapshot.

## Integrated changes

- Startup: localized controller illustrations follow the route overview; back navigation preserves the language/route gate. Movement remains locked until practice begins. The three startup guide images now include the right-controller B callout and Continue / Restart / Exit menu explanation.
- Educational debrief: six selectable rows open illustrated explanations and cautions in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese (18 images). The old recorded/not-recorded display is removed; checkpoint data remains. Back, restart and exit are retained.
- Modal placement: the review, B menu and elevator warning share a fixed player pose and upright world anchor, centred 1.65 m above the floor and 1.45 m forward. Natural headset rotation remains available.
- In-exercise menu: right B opens Continue / Restart / Exit; restart and exit require confirmation. Simulation and audio pause; inventory, escort and checkpoint state are preserved on Continue. Spraying stops and requires trigger release before resuming.
- Elevator safety: all four call panels open a localized fire warning with a required acknowledgement before movement resumes.
- Smoke: the 401 passage has a localized grey eye-level haze in addition to ceiling smoke. Its bounds stay on 4F; stair smoke profiles are unchanged.
- Smoke sign: one non-colliding three-language card at the smoke-zone centre faces Block B, with teal heading/frame, gold left-trigger keycap and peripheral attention cue. It asks the player to wet and raise the towel before entering. It does not block rays.
- Build support: retain the required UI shader in GraphicsSettings; preserve Unity 2022.3.62f3 and Windows PC VR/OpenXR targeting.

- Cleanup: removed the unused legacy Refined lighting directory (6.1 MiB) after checking external GUID references and Unity dependencies. Protected resources and metadata remained unchanged. Local regenerable caches/temporary files were cleared separately; see [cleanup details](../FinalProjectCleanup.md).

The owner subsequently accepted the final result.
