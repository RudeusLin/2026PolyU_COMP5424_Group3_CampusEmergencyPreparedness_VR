Upper-floor task and door hint update (2026-11-22)

Covers the active 4F/3F journey: alarm; towel pickup, wetting and raising; Stair A descent and rerouting; 3F corridor guidance; extinguisher pickup/equip/pin removal/spraying; Stair B descent; and all upper-floor doorway prompts. The intermediate stair landings are included. Placements and frames are shared by all three languages. The radial fire countdown remains at its fire target as a progress display.

CampusUpperFloorHintPlacement.cs searches upright solid wall/pier surfaces, validates the full card footprint and viewer sightlines to its centre/corners, checks protruding obstacles, and scores nearby full-width and compact cards. It synchronizes moved door colliders before searching. Normal height is 1.6m above the relevant landing; the existing authored interaction stations retain their tuned positions. Door-header support is a fallback for constrained layouts. Door poses are cached by approach side and neighbouring task-card position. If only one card location is available, the close-door instruction temporarily takes priority over the route card instead of drawing both on top of each other.

CampusXRWorldHints.cs preserves the authored station anchors after item pickup, applies mounted poses to route/fire states, and enables the existing CampusHintAttention thin frame and side-view cue for both task and door cards. Compact cards use 380x480 UI units (0.475x0.6m at the existing canvas scale), preserving font size while wrapping content.

Validation: 116 checks passed for 20 task-display states and 14 upper-floor doors approached from both sides. Full-route regression: 196 passed checks. Screenshots were visually reviewed, including open-door leaf occlusion; wall and corner line-of-sight checks were strengthened accordingly. These are simulated Editor checks; headset readability and comfort remain for human confirmation.
