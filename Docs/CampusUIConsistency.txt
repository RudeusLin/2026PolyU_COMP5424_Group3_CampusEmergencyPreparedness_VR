Complete campus UI presentation update (2026-11-22)

Scope
- Remaining 2F/1F task and door cards, all exit-fire states at main/A/B exits, side-exit outdoor guidance and the assembly objective.
- Refuge confirmation label now stays on the side wall, separately from the escort card. The floor target, downward arrow, colors and A confirmation are retained. Completion disables its attention cue while retaining the result frame.
- Startup language/briefing/map views, the classroom route image, emergency phone, debrief and head-relative status share quiet frames. Interactive dialogs keep their existing usable positions and button hit areas; the status HUD keeps Y/H visibility controls.
- Fire progress retains its target-relative ring/countdown and now frames its control caption.

Placement and presentation
CampusUpperFloorHintPlacement.cs was renamed CampusHintSurfacePlacement.cs, retaining its .meta GUID, and expanded to all floors. Doorway and route cards use the existing footprint, visibility and side-cache checks. Lower-floor candidates also avoid printed plaques; the renderer cache refreshes after scene reload/restart.
Exit cards search around their actual doorway centres so the player reads toward the exit. Unwalled exterior legs use simple noncolliding board supports (CampusHintStand.cs). The assembly instruction uses the existing assembly signpost.
CampusHintAttention.cs now supports frame-only displays and inherits each card's graphic material, including overlay exit/dialog cards. The grazing-angle information badge sits above the text rectangle, not over its content. It is noninteractive and obeys distance/occlusion rules.
The three supplied route images remain byte-for-byte unchanged; only their enclosing frame is added.
