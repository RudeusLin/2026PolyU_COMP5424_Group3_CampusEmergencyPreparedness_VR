# Four-floor smoke passage checkpoint

The XR teleport controller checks the entire feet-to-destination segment against a nonphysical smoke checkpoint at X -12.25 to -9.5, Z -1.65 to 1.65, Y 10.8 to 13.3. This is the 4F corridor west of the 401 washroom, on the route to Stair A. It deliberately leaves the towel and tap accessible without protection. Ceiling smoke elsewhere remains atmosphere rather than a mandatory checkpoint.

Entry and crossing require the towel equipped, wet and raised. X selects the towel; left trigger toggles its raised state. Having acquired or previously raised a towel does not suffice. Invalid targeting uses the existing invalid teleport indicator and localized feedback in all three languages.

If the towel is lowered or switched away inside the checkpoint, teleporting deeper or through to the far side is rejected. Teleporting out toward the most recent safe entry side remains available. Raising the wet towel restores normal traversal immediately. This gates ray teleport locomotion, not physical room-scale walking.
